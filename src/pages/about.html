About Hadon

Built for builders. Engineered for speed.

Hadon for business was founded on a simple observation: modern tech companies shouldn’t have to choose between slow domestic agencies and disconnected offshore outsourcing.

Our origin & story

A small senior pod can out-build an agency floor.

We started Hadon with a core team of senior builders—a product designer who understands high-conversion user psychology, a senior fullstack engineer who writes bulletproof code, and operations leaders who understand the friction of scaling global teams.

We recognized that the modern software landscape has changed. With the emergence of modern developer tooling and AI-native workflows, a small, highly disciplined senior pod can out-build an entire traditional agency floor.

Today, from our engineering center in India and our operations hub in Manila, we partner with visionary teams in the US, Europe, and Southeast Asia to bring digital products to life.
