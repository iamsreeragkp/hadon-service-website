How does the 14-day risk-free pilot work?

When you begin an engagement with a dedicated engineer or designer, your first 14 days serve as a working trial. If you feel the technical output, communication, or cultural fit doesn’t meet your expectations, you can cancel immediately. You will owe nothing.

How do we communicate day-to-day?

Our team embeds directly into your existing communication stack. We join your Slack or Discord, participate in your daily standups via Google Meet or Zoom, push pull requests to your GitHub or GitLab, and track tasks in Linear, Jira, or Notion.

Who owns the code and intellectual property?

You own 100% of all code, designs, documentation, and intellectual property from the moment it is created. All contracts include strict global IP assignment clauses and comprehensive Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).

How do we pay you internationally?

We make international payments seamless and local. Through our multi-currency accounts (Wise Business), US clients can pay via domestic ACH or wire, European clients via SEPA, and Southeast Asian clients via FAST/local SGD transfer. We also support corporate contractor platforms like Deel.

Can we hire you for a fixed-scope project instead of a monthly retainer?

Yes. If you have a well-defined scope for a new MVP, dashboard redesign, or platform modernization, our Turnkey MVP Studio delivers fixed-scope projects with a guaranteed 4 to 8-week delivery timeline. Terms are scoped on a discovery call.
